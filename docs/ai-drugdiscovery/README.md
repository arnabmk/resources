# Artificial Intelligence in Drug Discovery

## Why this matters?
Well, Like every other fields, the traditional drug discovery pipeline has been undergoing a significant changes with the integration of Artificial intelligence. 
Big Pharmaceuticals are now heavily using AI and collaborating with other AI companies to speed up and automate their workflows. Yes, You aren't in science fiction 
movies as "A generative AI-discovered TNIK inhibitor for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis: a randomized phase 2a trial" was the tittle of a paper published in nature 
medicine last year (2025)
